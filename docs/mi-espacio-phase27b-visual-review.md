# Mi Espacio · Phase 27B · Visual acceptance protocol

Automated Chromium checks cover 390, 768 and 1366 px screens, including a short 390×720 viewport, reduced motion, opening and returning from Calendar, Directory and Research, and document overflow. These checks do not certify clinical production or visual quality from actual signed-in accounts.

For the final institutional acceptance, compare **redacted** recordings or screenshots of department head, clinician, resident and biomedical engineer at the same breakpoints. Inspect heading rhythm, panel density, context preservation, focus and scroll return, hidden controls, mobile input keyboard behavior, sticky-header collision, motion jitter, readable contrast and data truthfulness. Do not merge or store recordings in the repository.

A reviewer should log each defect with exact role, viewport, trigger and expected/observed behavior, and separately note any unresolved defect before authorizing deployment. Treat CI as synthetic evidence only. Never claim those manual inspections occurred without recordings and authorized access.
